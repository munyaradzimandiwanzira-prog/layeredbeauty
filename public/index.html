Questions

Good to know.

Is submitting the intake a confirmed booking?add

No. The intake is a request for assessment. Your wig is reviewed first, then you receive a written quote. A booking is confirmed only once you approve the quote and the deposit is arranged.

Why does a revamp cost more than a wash?add

Revamp services are priced around the time, condition assessment, careful product work and finished styling involved — not just a wash. After seeing your unit, we recommend the service that makes sense and give you a clear quote before we begin.

Do you work on synthetic wigs?add

The menu is designed for quality human-hair units. Synthetic and blended units may need a separate assessment, and some may be declined if the fibre can't take the service safely.

What if my wig needs more than the package covers?add

Extra detangling, damaged lace, colour correction and reconstruction are assessed separately. Any additional work, shipping or price change is confirmed with you before it goes ahead.

How do I get my wig to you and back?add

Choose drop-off and pickup, or a shipped return (charged separately), on the intake form. If you're unsure, select “Let's discuss”.

Will you post photos of my wig?add

Only with your permission. Marketing consent is optional, not required for service, and specific images are confirmed with you separately before anything is shared.
